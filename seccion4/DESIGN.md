## Brand & Style

This design system is built directly from the corporate identity of an established promotional solutions and service partner. The brand personality embodies precision, collaborative trust, technical reliability, and structured vitality. It balances institutional sobriety with energetic operational accents, targeting enterprise procurement officers, brand managers, and corporate campaign coordinators.

The UI avoids decorative clutter and embraces a **Corporate / Modern** aesthetic, enriched by purposeful functional color-coding. Visual clarity is achieved through crisp typographic contrast, clean white foundations, and structured card architectures. The design evokes dependability, effortless navigation, and professional dynamism, translating the brand's signature four-dot accent system into practical service tiers, category tags, and operational states across all interfaces.

## Colors

The color palette translates the brand mark's primary identity and its four dot accents into functional digital interfaces:

- **Primary Institutional Sky Blue (`#5BA4D9`):** Represents the core brand anchor ("SOPORTE"). Applied to primary interactive elements, active links, focused navigation points, and prominent calls to action.
- **Secondary Cobalt Blue (`#3B6EA8`):** Sourced from the second brand dot. Serves as a deep complement to the primary sky blue, ideal for secondary buttons, key metrics, and formal institutional elements.
- **Tertiary Olive Apple Green (`#7BA03A`):** Derived from the third brand dot. Signifies operational success, verified workflows, inventory availability, and affirmative status indicators.
- **Neutral Charcoal Slate (`#4D4D4D`):** Extracted from the typographic baseline ("PROMOCIONAL") and the first brand dot. Anchors body typography, strong outlines, and deep neutral structural accents.
- **Accent Terracotta Ochre (`#BA5D2C`):** Extracted from the fourth brand dot. Dedicated to alerts, priority highlights, pending notifications, and high-impact category chips.

### Surface & Contrast Philosophy
- **Base Canvas:** Pure pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) ensures clarity and optimal contrast.
- **Subtle Surface Foundations:** Cool neutral whites and slate tints (`#F8FAFC` and `#F1F5F9`) define container modules and subtle table rows.
- **Text & Readability:** Primary body copy is set in `#2B2D31`, headings in `#1E2024`, and muted metadata in `#6E737B`, ensuring full WCAG AAA legibility against white and tinted cards.

## Typography

The typography directly adopts the visual character of the brand identity: clean, balanced geometric construction with deliberate weight tension. **Plus Jakarta Sans** is employed across all hierarchy levels to reflect the contemporary geometric forms of the logotype.

### Typographic Contrast Model
The logotype pairs a lighter sky blue uppercase heading ("SOPORTE") over a sturdy charcoal baseline ("PROMOCIONAL"). This principle governs the editorial hierarchy:
- Primary display banners and hero labels utilize `Light (300)` or `Regular (400)` weights with generous tracking to convey clarity and openness.
- Section tags, subheadings, and actionable labels transition into `SemiBold (600)` and `Bold (700)` weights to establish structural authority and scanability.
- Uppercase transformations are reserved for small category tags, tabs, and status badges (`label-sm` and `label-md`).

## Layout & Spacing

This design system implements an adaptive fluid 12-column grid anchored to an 8px spacing rhythm (with 4px sub-increments for fine components).

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers cap at a max-width of 1280px for standard dashboards and 1440px for broad catalog views.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `0.75rem` gutters.

Spacing tokens (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) define the cadence of cards and internal element grouping. Tight associations (such as badges paired with titles) utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while modular dashboard sections separate with `space-lg` and `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **ambient layering** and **low-contrast neutral outlines**, ensuring the crisp white aesthetic remains prominent without heavy visual drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` or cool tinted neutral `#F8FAFC`. Used for foundational dashboard viewports and canvas backdrops.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Static Containers):** Solid `#FFFFFF` background bound by a 1px solid hairline border in `#E5E9EE`. Elevation shadow is minimal: `0 1px 3px rgba(77, 77, 77, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Popovers):** Raised modules on hover, select dropdowns, and segmented menus carry a softened ambient shadow: `0 4px 14px rgba(77, 77, 77, 0.08)` paired with an inner border `#DCE2EA`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Menus):** Positioned above a dim scrim (`rgba(30, 32, 36, 0.45)`). Card depth relies on `0 12px 28px rgba(77, 77, 77, 0.14)` with crisp boundary clipping.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** configuration, balancing geometric structure with modern approachability.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Standard buttons, text inputs, dropdown menus, and standard card containers.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Primary hero cards, dashboard widget containers, and modals.
- **Full Radius (Pill / 9999px):** Status badges, categorical tags, chips, and the circular quaternary dot markers referencing the logotype's 4 brand dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with Sky Blue `#5BA4D9`, white text, 8px radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding, typography `label-lg`. Hover transitions to `#4A92C6` with subtle translateY(-1px). Focus ring: 3px `#5BA4D9` at 30% opacity.
- **Secondary:** Filled with Cobalt Blue `#3B6EA8` or an outline variant with 1.5px border `#4D4D4D`, text `#4D4D4D`, and transparent background that shifts to `#F1F5F9` on hover.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Transparent background with Sky Blue or Charcoal text, padding `0.5rem 1rem`, for inline list actions and dismissable modals.

### Categorical Badges & Chips (The 4-Dot Brand Matrix)
Badges feature an authentic pill silhouette (`rounded-full`) with a soft tinted background (10% opacity) and solid foreground text, accompanied by a signature 8px circular dot indicating category or operational state:
- **Dot 1 (Charcoal `#4A4A4A`):** Core administration, archived assets, standard specifications.
- **Dot 2 (Cobalt `#3B6EA8`):** Corporate solutions, active orders, technical processing.
- **Dot 3 (Olive Green `#7BA03A`):** In stock, completed validation, approved campaign deliverables.
- **Dot 4 (Terracotta `#BA5D2C`):** Urgent review, promotional spotlight, pending attention.

### Tabs & Navigation
- **Segmented Line Tabs:** Clean horizontal baseline border in `#E5E9EE`. Active tab indicated by a 2.5px bottom highlight bar in `#5BA4D9` with SemiBold text.
- **Pill Tabs:** Contained in a light `#F1F5F9` track; active item lifts with `#FFFFFF` background, subtle elevation shadow, and `#4D4D4D` typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 42px, radius 8px, 1px border `#DCE2EA`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus changes border to `#5BA4D9` with a 3px soft blue glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px dimensions, border 1.5px `#4D4D4D`. When checked, filled with `#5BA4D9` containing a crisp white geometric checkmark.

### Cards & Modules
- Standard cards feature `#FFFFFF` fill, 12px or 16px internal padding, and a 1px border `#E5E9EE`.
- Header areas neatly integrate a service dot alongside the title to preserve immediate brand alignment throughout large tabular data and catalog grids.
